# Kwendi Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Kwendi is a software, educational, and cultural project intended to provide a respectful environment for development, learning, collaboration, and exchange of ideas.

We are committed to maintaining a community where people can participate respectfully and where technical, educational, and cultural discussions can take place without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in Kwendi activities.

---

## 2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions related to the Kwendi project, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Code reviews
- Project-related comments
- Project documentation
- Official Kwendi communication channels
- Meetings or events organized by Kwendi
- Private communication when it is directly related to a Kwendi collaboration or project activity

Project maintainers, contributors, collaborators, invited participants, sponsors, partners, and other participants are expected to follow this Code of Conduct when participating in Kwendi activities.

---

## 3. Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate clearly and professionally.
- Assume good intentions while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Respect the cultural and educational purpose of Kwendi.
- Give appropriate credit to other people's work.
- Respect intellectual-property rights.
- Protect confidential information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project when appropriate.
- Accept reasonable corrections and feedback.
- Ask questions when clarification is needed rather than making unnecessary assumptions.

Technical disagreement is completely acceptable.

Disrespectful behavior is not.

---

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within Kwendi activities:

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Personal attacks.
- Threats or deliberate attempts to frighten others.
- Discriminatory comments or behavior.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate humiliation of another participant.
- Repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sharing confidential project information without authorization.
- Impersonating another contributor or maintainer.
- Deliberately disrupting project activities.
- Maliciously submitting code, content, or reports intended to damage the project.
- Deliberate plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Using project resources without authorization.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

---

## 5. Examples

### Acceptable

> "I think this implementation could cause problems when the application is offline. Could we test this case before merging?"

This is a technical disagreement expressed constructively.

### Not acceptable

> "This is a stupid implementation. You clearly don't know what you're doing."

This attacks the person rather than addressing the technical issue.

### Acceptable

> "I found a possible issue with the lesson content. Here are the sources I used to verify it."

### Not acceptable

> Deliberately submitting incorrect cultural or educational information and refusing to correct it after the issue has been identified.

---

## 6. Project Ownership and Authority

Kwendi is a proprietary project.

Participation in the community does not grant ownership of Kwendi or its intellectual property.

Maintainers and authorized project owners may make decisions concerning:

- Project direction
- Code acceptance
- Content
- Releases
- Access permissions
- Collaborations
- Repository management
- Security
- Licensing
- Branding

Respectful disagreement with a project decision is allowed.

Harassment or retaliation because of a project decision is not.

---

## 7. Reporting a Violation

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the Kwendi project owner or an authorized maintainer.

Whenever possible, include:

- A description of what happened.
- The date and approximate time.
- Where the incident occurred.
- The people involved.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or other evidence.
- Any additional context that may help understand the situation.

Please do not publicly expose private information when reporting an incident.

### Security-related reports

Security vulnerabilities or exposed credentials should **not** be reported publicly through GitHub Issues.

They should be communicated privately to the project owner or through the project's designated security-reporting channel.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.

Information from a report will only be shared when necessary to investigate the situation, protect participants, or take appropriate action.

No participant should publicly disclose private information about a person involved in a report.

---

## 9. Response to Violations

The project owner or authorized maintainers may take appropriate action depending on the circumstances and severity of a violation.

Possible actions include:

1. A private warning.
2. A request to stop the behavior.
3. Removal of inappropriate comments or content.
4. Temporary restriction from participating in project discussions.
5. Temporary suspension of project access.
6. Removal from a collaboration.
7. Termination of contributor or collaborator authorization.
8. Permanent restriction from project activities.
9. Reporting the matter to an appropriate platform or authority when necessary.

The response may depend on the severity, frequency, context, and impact of the behavior.

Serious violations may result in immediate removal of access without a prior warning.

---

## 10. Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith, participates in an investigation, or supports another participant making a report is not permitted.

A person should not be punished simply for raising a legitimate concern.

False reports made deliberately to harm another person may themselves be considered a violation.

---

## 11. Sponsors, Partners, and Organizations

Sponsors, partners, companies, organizations, and representatives participating in Kwendi activities are also expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

Financial sponsorship, partnership, employment, or organizational status does not provide an exemption from these standards.

Likewise, participation in Kwendi does not grant any sponsor or partner ownership or control over the project unless expressly established by a separate written agreement.

---

## 12. Contributors and Maintainers

Contributors and maintainers have the same basic expectations of respectful conduct.

Maintainers additionally have a responsibility to:

- Apply project rules consistently.
- Review reports seriously.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid abusing project authority.
- Explain decisions when appropriate.
- Take reasonable steps to keep project spaces safe and constructive.

---

## 13. Cultural Respect

Kwendi has an educational and cultural purpose.

Participants should approach Angolan cultural and language-related subjects with care and respect.

Discussions involving language, traditions, history, culture, or educational material should prioritize accuracy, context, and respectful dialogue.

Disagreement about cultural or educational content is allowed and encouraged when expressed constructively.

---

## 14. Good-Faith Participation

Kwendi welcomes honest questions, constructive criticism, technical disagreement, and good-faith discussion.

Not knowing something is not a violation.

Making a mistake is not automatically a violation.

What matters is how participants respond when an issue is identified and whether they make a reasonable effort to correct harmful or inappropriate behavior.

---

## 15. Contact

For conduct-related concerns, contact the Kwendi project owner or an authorized maintainer through the official contact method associated with the project.

For sensitive matters, please use a private communication channel rather than a public GitHub Issue or Pull Request.

---

## 16. Updates

This Code of Conduct may be updated as the Kwendi community and project evolve.

Changes may be made to clarify expectations, improve reporting procedures, or address new situations.

The current version published in this repository is the applicable version for Kwendi project activities.

---

## 17. Acknowledgment

By participating in Kwendi activities, contributors, collaborators, maintainers, sponsors, partners, and other participants are expected to respect this Code of Conduct.

Thank you for helping make Kwendi a respectful, constructive, and collaborative project.
